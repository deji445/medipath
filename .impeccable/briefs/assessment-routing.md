# Assessment and hospital routing

## Direction contract

**THESIS:** Turn an uncertain health concern into a calm, explainable next step without pretending to diagnose. Refuse the category-default chatbot transcript and generic facility directory; each screen exposes one decision and the reason behind it.

**OWN-WORLD:** Extend MediPath’s Calm Triage Desk: cool slate canvas, white paper panels, care-teal progress and primary actions, thin clinical borders, 12–16px corners, Instrument Sans, and amber or red only for genuine urgency.

**STORY:** The patient answers one short question at a time, sees an immediate interrupt if a warning sign appears, receives plain-language urgency guidance, then chooses among only the facilities that can safely receive that level of concern. Selection stays private until explicit consent.

**FIRST VIEWPORT:** Assessment opens on one question, semantic progress, the concern being assessed, and a single full-width action. Routing opens with the selection task and privacy boundary before the ranked list; each facility shows capability, evidence, and one unmistakable action. The signature interaction is the question-by-question transition with an immediate emergency branch.

**FORM:** Established-surface extension, first and only applicable structure; the existing IntakeLayout and patient-panel composition are inherited. Seed key: `not-applicable-local-extension` because Impeccable’s new-work contract exempts local additions from a concept tournament.

**FINISH:** unreviewed and undocumented is unfinished; this build ends with the finish review, the verdict, DESIGN.md, and every shipping raster carrying its provenance
