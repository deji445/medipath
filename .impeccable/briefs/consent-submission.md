# Consent and hospital submission

## Direction contract

**THESIS:** Make sharing feel deliberate, legible, and reversible until the final action. Refuse the category-default legal wall of text and the vague “submit” button; show the exact destination, the exact data groups, and the consequence of consent.

**OWN-WORLD:** Extend MediPath’s Calm Triage Desk without creating a new visual identity: quiet white clinical paper, cool slate canvas, Care Teal for the trusted next action, thin borders, 12–16px corners, Instrument Sans, and semantic red only for real safety warnings.

**STORY:** The patient arrives from a saved hospital choice, verifies the destination and disclosure scope, grants one explicit hospital-sharing consent, then receives a durable submission reference. The selected hospital sees the immutable snapshot in its admissions queue and can advance its operational status without changing the patient’s submitted facts.

**FIRST VIEWPORT:** The consent review opens with the hospital destination, the privacy boundary, and a concise list of what will and will not be shared before the consent control. The receipt opens with confirmed delivery, reference, destination, and the honest instruction that MediPath does not replace emergency care. The signature interaction is the single final consent action changing a private intake into an immutable hospital submission.

**FORM:** Established-surface extension in Operate mode. The existing IntakeLayout, patient-panel composition, dashboard queue, and CaseSummaryPanel are inherited. Seed key: `not-applicable-local-extension`; the task adds a bounded step to a confirmed journey and does not require a concept tournament.

**FINISH:** unreviewed and undocumented is unfinished; this build ends with responsive captures, one mechanical detector pass, a fresh finish review, and an ordinary-extension documentation audit.
