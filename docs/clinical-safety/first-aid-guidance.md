# Emergency first-aid guidance

MediPath shows basic first-aid steps only after it has told the user to call Ghana's emergency number, 112. The steps do not replace instructions from the emergency dispatcher or a trained responder.

## Supported situations

- Clearly described external bleeding from an arm, hand, leg, or foot.
- A suspected closed fracture of an arm, hand, leg, or foot.

The guidance is deliberately limited to direct pressure, keeping an injured limb still, avoiding unnecessary movement, and following the 112 dispatcher's instructions.

## Excluded situations

MediPath does not show the simplified limb guidance for shootings, stabbings, amputations, open fractures, visible bone, or injuries involving the head, neck, spine, hip, pelvis, chest, abdomen, eyes, or ears. Internal bleeding descriptions such as vomiting or coughing blood are also excluded. These cases still receive immediate emergency escalation.

## Clinical review

This prototype wording is based on public first-aid guidance from the Ghana National Ambulance Service, the American Red Cross, and the NHS. A Ghana-licensed clinician and emergency-care professional must review and approve the rules and copy before production use.
